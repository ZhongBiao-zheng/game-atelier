and upstream LICENSE.md. The complete unmodified corresponding source archive and original

Run build_video_encoder.sh on the corresponding platform to rebuild. configuration.txt records
the actual toolchain and configure invocation. No external codec libraries or zlib are linked.
Apple VideoToolbox and Windows Media Foundation are provided by the operating system.
Windows uses MinGW-w64 CRT (https://www.mingw-w64.org/license/) and GCC runtime libraries
